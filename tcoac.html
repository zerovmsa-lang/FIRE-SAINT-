T. C. O. A. C.

Basic Information

Full Title: T. C. O. A. C.

Meaning: The Creator of All Creators

Role: Supreme Creator

Residence: Origin Realm

Authority: Absolute

Description

The supreme creator of every being, every realm, and every creator within FIRE-SAINT. He stands at the beginning of all beginnings and the end of all ends.

Every law, concept, and hierarchy ultimately traces its existence back to him.

Trivia

• Creator of Sim Te Lenna.

• Creator of the Realm of the Writers.

• Original holder of the Chaos Flame and the Creation Flame.

• Bestowed the title "The Fire Saint" upon Ozai.
