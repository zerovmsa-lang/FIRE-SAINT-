The Story of the Fire Saint

Every legend has an origin. Before the realms. Before the flames. Before existence itself. This archive records the history of the Fire Saint, beginning from the very first moment of creation.

The Absolute Creator

T. C. O. A. C. (The Creator of All Creators) is the creator of all beings, creators, and overpowered beings alike. Everything that exists ultimately originates from him.

Calling him "absolute" is still insufficient to describe the reality of his existence. He is the beginning of all beginnings and the end of all ends. He is almighty praise him

To claim superiority over T. C. O. A. C. is not merely impossible—it is considered a sin.

He resides within the Origin Realm, where all existence ultimately traces its beginning.

Origin Space

Origin Space is not truly a space. It is the absence of everything and nothing—the absence of even absolute nothingness. It is silent beyond description and stretches without end.

Within this indescribable space, T. C. O. A. C. first emerged.

His emergence clashed with Origin Space, creating an intense undesceibable and undefineable heat that took the form of the First Flame, later known as the Chaos Flame.

As he witness the flame burning and eating away an actuality of the space which no language can describe,its heat never even touching the space he immediately stop it and recognizing its future significance, T. C. O. A. C. brought the flame under his control and preserved it for the future.

Creation of Sim Te Lenna

After a long time, T. C. O. A. C. created the realm known as Sim Te Lenna together with countless higher beings. During this era, every inhabitant lived together in unity beneath the Creator.

Emergence of Evils

As ages passed, evil was born within Sim Te Lenna. The Seven Deadly Sins—Pride, Greed, Lust, Envy, Gluttony, Wrath, and Sloth—appeared together with their followers.

Driven by envy toward the Creator's love for the lowest class of higher beings, they began exploiting the realm and sowing conflict throughout Sim Te Lenna.

Although T. C. O. A. C. possessed the power to erase them instantly, he chose not to. Knowing their rebellion long before it happened, he still loved his creations and refused to destroy them.

Only time would reveal what the Creator was truly preparing.

The Great War

The war between the forces of good and evil marked the true beginning of Ozai's journey. Although he belonged to the lowest power class within Sim Te Lenna, he became a soldier of the True Heaven, a position few believed someone of his rank could ever achieve.

For countless eons the war continued without end. Neither side could truly perish, for only T. C. O. A. C. possessed the authority to judge or erase the higher beings.

Despite being looked down upon because of his low status, Ozai continued smiling and carrying out his duties without arrogance or hatred.

The Meeting with Nyra Solwein

During a meeting in the Auspicious Hall of the True Heaven, Ozai was accused of leaking military strategies to the enemy before the Empress of the Nine Heavens, Nyra Solwein.

Rather than pleading for mercy, Ozai's childish personality took over. He boldly approached the Empress, slapped her across the face, kissed her, and escaped before anyone could react.

The hall fell into complete silence. Nyra's expression finally cracked as anger, shock, and emotions she had never experienced before overwhelmed her.

The Fire Saint's Awakening

As the Great War neared its end, T. C. O. A. C. suddenly transported Ozai into the Origin Space without warning.

Confused by the sudden change, Ozai questioned where he was. Before him stood the Creator himself, who had watched the boy's unwavering innocence throughout the endless war.

Believing Ozai worthy of carrying a greater responsibility, T. C. O. A. C. presented him with the Creation Flame.

Before the Creator could explain further, the First Flame—the Chaos Flame—left his control and flew directly into Ozai's chest, as though recognizing a familiar existence.

Seeing this unexpected event, T. C. O. A. C. smiled and bestowed upon Ozai a new title:

The Fire Saint.

Ozai was then returned to the battlefield, carrying a destiny that no other being knew.

The Battle Against the Seven Deadly Sins

Wishing to keep his identity hidden, Ozai challenged the Seven Deadly Sins alone and drew them away from the battlefield into the Origin Space.

There, he unveiled the overwhelming power granted by both the Chaos Flame and the Creation Flame.

His first legendary technique became known as The Blazing Eyes, a gaze capable of burning even beings born from sin itself. To describe this technique, omniscence might be a joke to it, he knows their weakness, but dif not reveal it, and it seems to work just fine, burning them

When the Seven Sins we burned, they screamed in agonny they tried to retaliat but Ozai unleashed a concentrated beam of Chaos Flame that tore through and erased half of the never ending Origin Space without the heat ever making contact,words alone cannot describe this destruction. The technique would later be remembered as Chaos Beam.

Though all the seven deadly evil sins survived, their appearance did not even look living anymore

Finally to end their misery, Ozai created a realm within his own left eye using chaos flame and imprisoned every one of the Seven Deadly Sins inside it forever (his left eye was called True heaven) . This sealing technique became known as Seal of the Evils.

The Duel with T. C. O. A. C.

After sealing the Seven Deadly Sins, Ozai did something no being had ever dared to do.

He looked at T. C. O. A. C. and, with absolutely no malice, asked if they could spar. It wasn't a challenge born from pride—Ozai simply wanted a story to tell someday: "I sparred with the Creator Himself."

After restoring the damaged Origin Space, T. C. O. A. C. accepted.

Ozai unleashed every technique and every ounce of power he possessed, yet none of it could reach the Creator.

Heat Overload

At the edge of defeat, Ozai awakened his ultimate technique: Heat Overload.

His body became pure flame as the Chaos Flame and Vanity fused together. The heat became so intense that even the Origin Space, where absolute nothingness did not exist, began to disappear, it did not only erase or destroy, it makes the space unable to be repaired without the heat ever making contact.

For the first time ever... the hem of T. C. O. A. C.'s robe was burned. Within a fraction of a second, The unending realm of undescribable absence of notthingness was burned to never be repaired

But the Creator immediately stopped the technique before it could fully manifest and warned Ozai never to use it again.

The Wish

After the duel, Ozai voluntarily returned both the Chaos Flame and the Creation Flame.

He smiled and admitted that although being powerful had been fun, he believed the flames had already fulfilled their purpose.

Moved by Ozai's selflessness, T. C. O. A. C. granted him one wish.

Ozai wished only for the freedom to travel every realm, every world, and every point in time.

The Creator accepted.
