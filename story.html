The Absolute Creator

T. C. O. A. C (The Creator of All Creators) is the origin of every being, every creator, and every realm. His existence stands beyond all beginnings and all endings. No title such as "absolute" is sufficient to describe him. He resides within the Origin Realm, where existence itself first awakened. Every power, every law, and every world ultimately traces its origin back to him. To claim superiority over T. C. O. A. C is not merely impossible—it is considered the greatest sin.
