The Story of the Fire Saint

Every legend has an origin. Before the realms. Before the flames. Before existence itself. This archive records the history of the Fire Saint, beginning from the very first moment of creation.

The Absolute Creator

T. C. O. A. C. (The Creator of All Creators) is the creator of all beings, creators, and overpowered beings alike. Everything that exists ultimately originates from him.

Calling him "absolute" is still insufficient to describe the reality of his existence. He is the beginning of all beginnings and the end of all ends.

To claim superiority over T. C. O. A. C. is not merely impossible—it is considered a sin.

He resides within the Origin Realm, where all existence ultimately traces its beginning.

Origin Space

Origin Space is not truly a space. It is the absence of everything and nothing—the absence of even absolute nothingness. It is silent beyond description and stretches without end.

Within this indescribable void, T. C. O. A. C. first emerged.

His emergence clashed with Origin Space, creating an immeasurable heat that took the form of the First Flame, later known as the Chaos Flame.

Recognizing its future significance, T. C. O. A. C. brought the flame under his control and preserved it for the future.

Creation of Sim Te Lenna

After a long time, T. C. O. A. C. created the realm known as Sim Te Lenna together with countless higher beings. During this era, every inhabitant lived together in unity beneath the Creator.

Emergence of Evils

As ages passed, evil was born within Sim Te Lenna. The Seven Deadly Sins—Pride, Greed, Lust, Envy, Gluttony, Wrath, and Sloth—appeared together with their followers.

Driven by envy toward the Creator's love for the lowest class of higher beings, they began exploiting the realm and sowing conflict throughout Sim Te Lenna.

Although T. C. O. A. C. possessed the power to erase them instantly, he chose not to. Knowing their rebellion long before it happened, he still loved his creations and refused to destroy them.

Only time would reveal what the Creator was truly preparing.
