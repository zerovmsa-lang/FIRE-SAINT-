Flame System

The Sacred Flames

The Chaos Flame and the Creation Flame are the two sacred flames entrusted only by T. C. O. A. C. Although both flames once resided within Ozai during the final stage of the Great War, they possess completely different authorities and purposes.

Chaos Flame

The Chaos Flame is the first flame to ever exist. It came into existence when the emergence of T. C. O. A. C. clashed with the indescribable Origin Space. The immense heat manifested into a flame capable of burning and consuming the undescribable space where even absolute nothingness was absent.

Recognizing its future importance, T. C. O. A. C. immediately brought the flame under His authority and safeguarded it until the day it fulfilled its destined purpose.

Creation Flame

The Creation Flame was personally entrusted to Ozai by T. C. O. A. C. near the end of the Great War. Unlike the Chaos Flame, its authority lies in creation, restoration, healing and reconstruction. During Ozai's battle against the Seven Deadly Sins, it continuously restored his destroyed body, allowing him to continue fighting despite suffering injuries that should have ended the battle instantly.

Known Techniques

The Blazing Eyes

Ozai channels the entire Creation Flame into both of his eyes, producing an overwhelming gaze. To describe its perception as omniscience would be an understatement. The gaze burned even the Seven Deadly Sins, while simultaneously allowing Ozai to perceive their greatest weakness. From that moment onward, he exploited their inability to endure insults, provoking them into losing all composure.

Chaos Beam

Ozai amplifies and compresses the entire Chaos Flame into a single point before releasing a pure, unadulterated beam of heat. The attack ripped through the unending Origin Space, destroyed what was never even present, erased where even absolute nothingness was absent, and left destruction trapped within an endless state of ceasing itself.

Seal of the Evils

After defeating the Seven Deadly Sins, Ozai channeled the Chaos Flame into his left pupil, creating a separate realm known as True Hell within his own eye. Using a vortex of Chaos Flame, he imprisoned all Seven Deadly Sins inside that realm, where they remain sealed.

Heat Overload

The ultimate technique created by Ozai during his friendly duel with T. C. O. A. C. Ozai concentrated all of his vanity and the Chaos Flame into a single point while the Creation Flame burst from within him. His physical body transformed into pure flame, becoming intangible and unharmable. The resulting heat erased the never-ending Origin Space itself, a place where even absolute nothingness was absent, and for the first time ever, the heat reached and burned the hem of T. C. O. A. C. before the Creator forcibly shut the technique down. Had its full power manifested completely, even T. C. O. A. C. estimated He might only endure it for one or two seconds before absolute erasure.
