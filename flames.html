Flame System

The Sacred Flames

The Chaos Flame and the Creation Flame are the two most sacred flames in FIRE-SAINT. Both originated from the Origin Space and were entrusted only to those deemed worthy by T. C. O. A. C.

Chaos Flame

The first flame ever born. It came into existence when T. C. O. A. C. first emerged within the Origin Space. Its destructive power surpasses all ordinary concepts of heat, capable of burning even places where absolute nothingness does not exist.

Creation Flame

A sacred flame entrusted by T. C. O. A. C. Unlike the Chaos Flame, it embodies creation, restoration, and overwhelming vitality. Together, the two flames represent both destruction and creation.
