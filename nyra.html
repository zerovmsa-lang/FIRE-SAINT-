Nyra Solwein

Basic Information

Name: Nyra Solwein

Title: Empress of the Nine Heavens

Gender: Female

Height: 6'7"

Affinity: Frost

Species: Higher Being

Appearance

A tall and elegant woman with long silver hair, deep violet eyes, and a graceful yet commanding presence. She wears regal white-and-blue attire befitting the ruler of the Nine Heavens.

Personality

Calm, expressionless, authoritative, and respected by all. Beneath her icy exterior lies emotions she rarely reveals, especially after meeting Ozai.
