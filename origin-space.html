Origin Space

Origin Space is not truly a "space." It is the complete absence of everything and nothing—the absence of even absolute nothingness. It cannot be measured, described, or comprehended by any created existence.

It has no beginning, no end, no direction, and no concept of time. Silence itself fails to describe its nature.

The First Emergence

Within Origin Space, T. C. O. A. C. became the first existence to ever emerge.

His appearance clashed with the indescribable Origin Space, producing an immeasurable heat that manifested as the First Flame, later known as the Chaos Flame.

Foreseeing its importance, T. C. O. A. C. brought the Chaos Flame under His authority and preserved it until the day it would fulfill its purpose.

Significance

Origin Space is the first appearance of T. C. O. A. C., the Chaos Flame, and the beginning of all creation. Every realm and every hierarchy ultimately traces its existence back to this place.
