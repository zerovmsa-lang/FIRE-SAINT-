Cosmology

The cosmology of FIRE-SAINT is divided into a hierarchy of realms, each existing beyond the one below it. Every higher layer transcends the laws, concepts, and limitations of the realms beneath it.

Tier 3 — Omniverse

The lowest layer of the greater hierarchy. It contains countless universes, multiverses, megaverses, hyperverses, and every ordinary reality. All created worlds exist within this domain.

Tier 2 — Outside Existence

A realm beyond the Omniverse where the concepts of space, time, dimensions, and existence no longer apply. Beings dwelling here transcend ordinary creation and perceive lower realities as insignificant.

Tier 1 — Realm of the Writers

The domain where fictional realities are conceived and shaped. Every created story ultimately traces its origin to this realm, yet it remains part of the created hierarchy.

Tier 0 — Sim Te Lenna

The highest created realm, inhabited by the Higher Beings who faithfully serve T. C. O. A. C. No created realm exists above it.

Origin Space

Origin Space exists beyond the entire hierarchy and is not considered a tier. It is the absolute origin from which all creation ultimately traces back.

T. C. O. A. C.

The Creator of All Creators. The eternal and absolute source of everything known and unknown. Every realm, law, concept, and existence ultimately originates from T. C. O. A. C., who stands beyond all hierarchy.
